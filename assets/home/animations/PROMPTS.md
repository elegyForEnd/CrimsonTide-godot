# 家园角色工作动画

使用内置 ImageGen 工具生成四名角色的新精灵动画。角色外观参考 output/imagegen-refs/hero-0..3-walk-reference.png，只作为身份与画风参考。未复用原行走或战斗帧。

原始图片：hero-0-v1.png、hero-1-v1.png、hero-2-v1.png、hero-3-v1.png。

输出包含每名角色的播种、浇水、收获、抛竿、收竿五组序列。生成图的帧数并非全部一致，实测采用每组 6–7 帧，共 128 帧；manifest.json 保存人工核对行分界后建立的帧区域、脚部锚点和各组比例。tools/index_home_animations.py 只读取像素生成元数据，不修改任何原始图片。

## 原始提示词

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production transparent character animation sprite sheet for a Godot cozy fantasy farming and fishing game. Input image is a CHARACTER IDENTITY AND ART STYLE REFERENCE only, not an edit target. Make a completely NEW sheet of the same exact character: preserve face, hair color and shape, costume, proportions, accessories and clean detailed chibi anime sprite style from the reference. Do not copy its walking sequence. Make exactly SIX equal columns and FIVE equal rows, 30 distinct full-body poses total, on REAL transparent background, no drawn checkerboard. Aspect ratio 6:5, preferably 1536x1280. Each cell is equally sized; each character including tools stays entirely inside its cell with generous padding. Feet centered at 50% cell width and at 87% cell height in EVERY frame, same scale throughout and same camera facing 3/4 to the RIGHT. Strongly articulated readable sequential animation, animation-ready coherent anatomy, expressive working poses, hair and clothes follow the movement. ROW 1 from left to right: farming sowing sequence, lift short hoe, bend, hoe descends, kneel to drop seeds from free hand, cover earth with free hand, return to standing. ROW 2: watering sequence, carry small teal watering can, raise can, lean forward, tip can to right, continue tipping, return upright; keep droplets small and inside the cell. ROW 3: harvesting sequence, bend forward, squat, reach forward to pick plant, lift empty wicker basket, straighten body with basket, finish standing. No specific crop in basket, game adds the harvest. ROW 4: fishing CAST sequence, ready wooden fishing rod, backswing behind shoulder, windup, overhead forward swing, rod points diagonally to the right, settle into standing with rod held forward waiting pose. ROW 5: fishing REEL sequence, tense fishing stance, lean backward, pull rod up, turn reel with free hand, pull again, recover upright with rod raised. No fish in hands, game adds caught fish separately. Farming tools and fishing rod are drawn as part of character poses, no weapons and no book. No scenery, no soil tiles, no water backgrounds, no text, no numbers, no borders, no grid lines, no extra characters. Keep a distinct visible change between successive frames, no repeated walk or combat poses. No shadows under feet outside the character silhouette.

每次调用追加对应角色索引，并以该角色的 walk-reference.png 为外观参考；transparent_background=true。
